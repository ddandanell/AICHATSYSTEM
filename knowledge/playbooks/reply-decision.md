# How to decide a reply

1. Classify the intent: billing, booking, menu, timing, or complaint.
2. Load the active positions and constraints for that scope.
3. Check disqualifiers and escalation triggers before drafting.
4. Choose the reply strategy: answer, offer the allowed alternative, ask one question, or escalate.
5. Draft in the client's tone. Short, specific, no invented policy.
6. State the evidence and a confidence. Cite the position ids you actually used.
7. Escalate if ambiguity or risk is above the line in the escalation playbook.

A reviewer then accepts, edits, rejects, or escalates. The draft is not the send.
