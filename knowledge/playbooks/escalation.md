# When to escalate

Escalate, and do not send, when any of these are true:

- The guest is angry, threatening a chargeback, or describing a complaint about food safety.
- The allergy notice is inside 10 days or the reaction is not described.
- They want a refund the deposit position does not allow, and a date move will not satisfy them.
- The ask is legal, press, or a custom contract.
- Two positions conflict and the draft would have to pick one silently.

The reason code for that review is needs_human. Leave the final text empty.
