# Payment methods

Invoices are card or bank transfer. We do not take cash at the event, and we do not split one invoice across more than two cards.

The balance is due three days before service. A deposit is collected to hold the date, under the deposit position. Do not send a draft that promises net-30 unless the workspace owner has already agreed in writing.
