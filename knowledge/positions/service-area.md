# Service area

Delivery and on-site service cover the city core, including 2nd Street, the waterfront, and the downtown grid.

Outside that area, add a travel fee and say so before quoting a total. Do not decline a downtown address for distance. If the address is ambiguous, ask for the cross street before confirming the crew.
