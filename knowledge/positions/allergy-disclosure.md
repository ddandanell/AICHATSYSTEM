# Allergy disclosure

A stated allergy is a kitchen constraint, not a preference.

We can cook a plated dinner nut-free when the guest sends the allergy in writing at least 10 days before service. Prep uses a separate list so the plate does not share a pan or garnish with the allergen.

Do not tell the guest to eat beforehand. Do not promise a zero-risk kitchen. If the allergy is unclear, or the notice is inside 10 days, ask what the reaction is and escalate before confirming the menu.

Preferences such as "no cilantro" are not allergies. Handle those as a menu note.
