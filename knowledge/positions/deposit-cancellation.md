# Deposits and cancellations

A booking deposit is non-refundable inside 14 days of the event.

Outside that window, return the deposit in the original form of payment within 10 business days.

Inside the window, offer one date move. The deposit can transfer to a new date booked within 6 months. Do not offer cash back, a partial refund, or a credit that expires sooner than 6 months.

If the guest count drops but the event date stays inside 14 days, the deposit still stands. Quote the new guest minimum separately. Do not treat a smaller party as a cancellation.
