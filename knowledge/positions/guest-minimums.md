# Guest count minimums

Seated service starts at $48 per guest. That price includes plated food and service staff. Do not accept a seated lunch or dinner below $48 to win the date.

A drop-off buffet can be $35 per guest when the client accepts disposable service and no on-site staff. Offer that when their budget is below the seated minimum. Do not pretend the drop-off is a seated meal.

Events inside 7 days need a confirmed headcount the same day to hold the date. Below 12 guests, propose a family-style menu instead of a full service team.
